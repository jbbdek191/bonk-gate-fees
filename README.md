## What you're actually buying

BONK launched on Solana on Christmas Day 2022, dropped by an anonymous group of 22 Solana builders as a reaction to the FTX aftermath. It's a dog-themed memecoin with a fixed supply of 100 trillion tokens, and half of that supply was airdropped to Solana community members rather than sold. There was no public or private sale, which is unusual for a token of its size and one reason it built a real holder base instead of a bag-holder base.

BONK isn't only a meme anymore. None of that makes BONK a safe asset. It's a memecoin. Price moves track community sentiment and social momentum more than any business model. Buy what you can afford to watch drop 30% in a week.

## Why the exchange matters more for BONK than for Bitcoin

Buying BTC on any large exchange is roughly the same experience. BONK is different for three reasons.

Liquidity is thinner. BONK trades in a fifth decimal place, so a large market order can chew through several price levels before it fills. On a thin book, a market order for a few thousand dollars can move the price against you before the order completes.

Network confusion is real. BONK exists on roughly ten chains. If you withdraw to an Ethereum address when the exchange sent ERC-20 BONK and you expected Solana, that's not a support ticket, that's gone.

Fee tiers hit harder on repeat trades. A 0.1% taker fee on one $500 buy is 50 cents. On a hundred trades of $500 during a volatile week, it's $50. That's the difference between a good month and a break-even one.

Gate lists BONK in both spot and derivatives markets, which is the main practical reason it comes up in this conversation.

## Spot versus perpetual on Gate

Gate carries BONK/USDT in both markets, and confusing them is a genuine way to lose money.

Spot means you own BONK outright. You buy it, it sits in your spot wallet, and the most you can lose is what you paid.

The perpetual contract was listed on Gate in January 2023, settled in USDT, with leverage available up to 20x. It's a derivative. You don't own the token, you hold a position that's marked to market, and 20x leverage on a memecoin that moves 10% in a day means a 10% move against you wipes the margin. Funding rates also get paid or received every few hours, which is an ongoing cost or credit that spot holders never see.

If your goal is owning BONK, use spot. If your goal is trading BONK's volatility with a defined risk budget, the perpetual exists for that and you should treat it as a separate skill.

## Getting your BONK off the exchange safely

Withdrawal is where the expensive mistakes live. Three things to check every time:

**Network.** Confirm which chain the exchange is sending on before you paste an address. Solana withdrawals need a Solana address. If the exchange supports BONK on multiple chains, the dropdown matters more than the address box.

**Contract address.** Copy the mint address from a source you trust and compare it against what your wallet shows. Fake BONK tokens with identical names and symbols exist on multiple chains. A wallet that displays the wrong contract address is showing you someone else's token.

**Test amount.** Send a small amount first. It costs one withdrawal fee and confirms the path works before you move the rest.

You can also leave BONK on the exchange. That's a trade-off, not a mistake: you avoid withdrawal fees and network risk, and you accept exchange custody risk. For a position you plan to sell within a week, staying put is often the rational choice.

## Mistakes that cost real money

**Buying BONK on the wrong chain.** There is real BONK on Solana, and there are wrapped versions and lookalikes elsewhere. Confirm the network before you send anything.

**Using market orders on thin books.** BONK's price has many decimal places and uneven depth. A limit order at a price you're happy with costs nothing if it doesn't fill and nothing extra if it does.

**Chasing a pump.** Memecoin price spikes are driven by attention. Buying the top of one is a well-documented hobby with poor returns.

**Turning on leverage to "make the move worth it".** 20x on a token with BONK's volatility is a coin flip with fees attached.

**Forgetting the tax and record trail.** Every trade on a centralised exchange generates a record. Export them before you need them.

**Skipping KYC to "start faster".** You'll hit the verification wall at withdrawal, usually at the worst moment.
